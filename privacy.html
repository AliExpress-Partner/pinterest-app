Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Information We Collect

This app connects to the Pinterest API using OAuth2. It stores only the access token required for the automation to publish pins on your behalf.

How We Use Your Information

The app fetches product information from AliExpress and posts it as pins to your Pinterest boards. No personal data is stored permanently.

Data Deletion

If you disconnect the app from n8n or revoke access from your Pinterest settings, all tokens are immediately invalidated and no further data is accessed.
